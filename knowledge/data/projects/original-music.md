## What Eric created

Original Music represents Eric’s long-running work as a composer, producer, performer, and audio engineer. The project links to his Niemo Audio catalog on SoundCloud, spanning classical and electronic work with original music and visual art. FL Studio is the primary production tool named for this collection.

## Implementation and interaction details

The linked SoundCloud page belongs to Niemo Audio and publishes “Small Talk (Build IV),” also displayed on SoundCloud as “Niemo - Small Talk b04.” It is a liquid drum-and-bass track released under a Creative Commons license and part of a broader profile accumulated over years. Listener counts and follower totals change over time, so they are intentionally not treated as stable résumé facts.

An independent Wikipedia editor uploaded the track to Wikimedia Commons. It is the audio example displayed on both the English Wikipedia article for Drum and bass and the English article for Liquid drum and bass. The Drum and bass article explicitly captions it as “Niemo - Small Talk Build IV.” This is a particularly meaningful and unusual public recognition because the example was selected by an editor rather than placed there by Eric.

The portfolio’s 2002–ongoing date indicates that this is a body of work rather than a single track. The linked track is a representative entry point, not the full extent of the catalog.

## What it demonstrates

This work demonstrates musical composition, arrangement, synthesis, sequencing, mixing, and long-term creative practice. It provides context for the custom sound and music present in Eric’s games and visualizations: audio is not merely a borrowed asset pipeline but another discipline he works in directly. “Small Talk” is liquid drum and bass, but the complete catalog is stylistically broader and should not be described as one genre.
