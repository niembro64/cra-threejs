# Eric Niemeyer — Hobbies and Personal Interests

## Music and composition

Eric is a concert violinist, piano composer, flamenco guitarist, electronic music producer, and DJ. He has performed with orchestras, bands, and quartets and has composed music for television and video games. His electronic track “Small Talk (Build IV),” released under Niemo, is the audio example used on the English Wikipedia articles for drum and bass and liquid drum and bass. His original-music project links listeners to a broader SoundCloud catalog.

## Audio-visual art and performance technology

Eric created audio visualizations with tools including After Effects, Premiere Pro, Photoshop, Illustrator, Blender, and OBS. In the Jackson and Mars live band, he also designed performance technology: a foot-controlled switch between live voice and lip-sync playback with matched intensity, plus multi-channel in-ear monitoring. He handed the system off by teaching both operation and rationale, and handled crowd or sound-engineer problems tactfully during live shows.

## Ultimate Frisbee

Eric plays competitive mixed-gender Ultimate Frisbee and has led league operations in South Korea and the United States. The hobby combines athletics with community building, scheduling, governance, safety, conflict resolution, and event operations. As Republic of Korea Ultimate executive manager, he coordinated captains, regional leaders, venues, sponsorship, equipment, budgets, and succession. He has also coordinated with Westchester Ultimate Disc.

## Games and technical hobbies

Many of Eric's personal projects sit between play and engineering. He builds game engines, physics experiments, strategy games, neural-network visualizations, collision labs, pathfinding demos, genetic algorithms, pixel art, embedded projects, and retro-computing experiments. Annihilation++ is the largest example: a native C++ real-time strategy game and custom engine designed for deterministic behavior across desktop CPU architectures.

## Other interests

Eric's interests include classical and flamenco music, electronic dance music, drum and bass, visual art, DJ equipment, game design, hardware, and cryptocurrency technology. He has hands-on experience with professional Bitcoin mining hardware and uses that background to understand blockchain mechanics rather than presenting cryptocurrency as his primary professional specialization.
