# Eric Niemeyer — Trivia and Personal Interests

> Canonical source: the public trivia section of niemo.io.

This document contains unusual facts, personal interests, music history, sports leadership, and hobbies associated with Eric Niemeyer.

## Trivia: Wikipedia-Famous

Niemo's track ["Small Talk (Build IV)"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Ars_Niemo_-_Small_Talk_Build_IV.ogg) holds a dignified place in the annals of electronic music. It's prominently featured on both the [Drum and Bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drum_and_bass) and [Liquid Drum and Bass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquid_drum_and_bass) Wikipedia pages. Uploaded to Wikimedia Commons by editor "Ftiercel" on April 15, 2012, it remains the single audio example shown on both English-language articles.

## Trivia: Ultimate Frisbee

Niemo plays a sport called Ultimate Frisbee, which is a competitive mixed-gender team sport. He was executive manager of [Republic of Korea Ultimate (ROK-U)](https://www.rokultimate.net/), the national ultimate frisbee league of South Korea, from 2015 - 2016. He coordinated regional leaders, captains, venues, budgets, and roughly 35 teams, then mentored his successor. He is currently a coordinator for [Westchester Ultimate Disc Inc (WUDI)](https://www.wudi.org/), a league in Westchester, NY.

## Trivia: Musician

Niemo is a versatile [musician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concertmaster) concert violinist, piano composer, flamenco guitarist, and electronic music DJ. He has performed with orchestras, bands, and quartets; composed for television and games; and designed live-audio systems for Jackson and Mars.

## Trivia: Crypto

Niemo began mining Bitcoin with professional-grade ASIC miners in 2017, when it was valued at just $1,000. Through this experience, he gained a deep understanding of blockchain and cryptocurrency, which he now leverages to advise friends and coworkers.
