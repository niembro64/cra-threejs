# Eric Niemeyer — Professional Work Overview

## Technical career throughline

Eric Niemeyer is a software engineer and engineering leader whose work connects machine learning, full-stack and mobile products, real-time media, industrial systems, and low-level simulation. At Venturetec and Sentien he leads product engineering across fraud detection, voice-agent retrieval, mobile applications, web systems, and client products. At Rockwell Automation and Maverick Technologies he built manufacturing execution system integrations joining plant-floor controls with databases and business systems. His personal engineering work includes a deterministic cross-architecture C++ real-time strategy engine.

## Venturetec and Sentien focus

Eric's current professional focus is leading engineering for Venturetec and Sentien. The work spans production machine-learning systems, React Native applications, web platforms, AWS infrastructure, data tooling, and technical demonstrations. SeniorSafe is the central ML example: a real-time phone-fraud product that classifies transcript turns and conversations, handles noisy speech-to-text data, retrieves relevant conversation evidence, and presents results through mobile and voice-agent experiences. Other major products include Audience AI and the Spray Integrity Program.

## Rockwell Automation and Maverick Technologies focus

Eric's industrial software experience centers on manufacturing execution systems. He integrated software with PLCs, robotics, operator interfaces, databases, ERP layers, and track-and-trace workflows for large manufacturers. Projects included automotive, tire, food, and controlled-material processes. This work required onsite process discovery, Java and database development, testing across legacy equipment, coordination with controls engineers, and careful management of changes in high-stakes production environments.

## Engineering range

Eric works across C++, C, Python, C#, Java, JavaScript, TypeScript, PHP, React, React Native, Vue, Angular, Node, Express, Laravel, Django, Flask, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, AWS, FFmpeg, and common embedded platforms. His machine-learning work includes PyTorch, neural text and audio classifiers, transformers, CNNs, RNNs, embeddings, semantic search, vector databases, RAG, and human-in-the-loop data systems. His projects also demonstrate game engines, deterministic simulation, pathfinding, graphics, signal processing, and browser-based experimentation.

## Leadership and delivery

Eric pairs hands-on engineering with requirements discovery, technical planning, mentorship, client communication, and delivery ownership. His recurring approach is to clarify what changed, record decisions and action items, assign clear ownership, explain schedule or scope consequences, and preserve a reproducible source state for releases. He has mentored engineers, interns, remote contributors, teachers, and organizational successors, and has presented technical systems to clients, investors, and mixed technical audiences.
